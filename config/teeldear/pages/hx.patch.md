- Command

  `Command Pallete: Command+Shift+P`
  `Duplicate selection: ".p`
- Insert methods
  
  `Start of line: I`
  `Start of selection: i`
  `End of line (append): A`
  `End of selection (append): a`
  `Open line above: O`
  `Open line below: o`
  `Paste after selection: p`
  `Paste before selection: P`
  `Replace with yanked text: R`
  `Toggle Comment: Command+/`

- select methods

  `Select full line: x`
  `Multiple entries: Visual mode, select the string, then hit * then hit n`
